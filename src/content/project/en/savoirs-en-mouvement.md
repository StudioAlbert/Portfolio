QIMEL is an association that brings image, culture and people together — photographs, neighbourhoods, cities, creations. Photographs and writings were gathered over a series of workshops. The Studio brought these two bodies of work together in an augmented reality application: the tablet gives printed images their companion words and offers the visitor a new layer of meaning.

## Tech

- Unity + AR Foundation
- Image recognition, text and image overlays
- Featured in an upcoming exhibition
